SEGMENT 05

What To Watch

Iowa State is the story at the top now that Notre Dame has a loss — ten straight wins for a program that started this chronicle's tracked history nowhere near this conversation, and with two players (Jaray Rask, Deshawn Bernard) now building real Heisman cases together. USC's debut season under Jesse Minter deserves the same attention: 8–1, built without a single Top 25 recruiting class, the clearest proof yet that development can outrun rankings.

And watch the bottom of the coaching carousel closest of all. Eastern Michigan's Chris Creighton is the only winless coach left in the country, joining a hot-seat board that has now grown to fifteen names in a single season.
