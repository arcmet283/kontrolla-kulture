Southern opens 2028 with a Team Rating of 75, up eight points from a year ago, and a roster that includes two 5-star transfers for the first time in program history. The preseason rank hasn't caught up yet — No. 89, identical to where last season started — but everything else about this roster looks different. This is the full preview: the schedule, the new faces, the record-setting recruiting class already on campus, and the questions still open after the biggest roster turnover this program has tracked.

SEGMENT 02

The Two 5-Stars: Flowers And Jones

Transfer · CB · No. 5 Overall Nationally
J'Vari Flowers

The player Southern lost as a high school recruit years ago, now arriving as the highest-rated player in program history — a 5-star and the No. 5 overall transfer add in the entire country. Full story in this cycle's Recruiting Chronicle.

Transfer · FS · From LSU
Ricardo Jones

A second 5-star transfer in the same class, already named 2nd Team All-Conference in the preseason before playing a down. Steps directly into the safety spot vacated by Byron Allen's graduation.

SEGMENT 03

The Record Signing Class Arrives

Alongside the two 5-star transfers, the 2028 high school class brings three 4-stars to campus — Will Johnston (SS), Jaelen McCracken (ATH/DE), and Danny Johnson (CB) — tying the 2026 class for the most 4-stars this program has ever signed in one cycle. The class finished ranked No. 36 nationally, a 27-spot jump from the year before.

SEGMENT 06

Heisman Watch: A Blank Slate

The 2028 Heisman Watchlist opens with five names, all at 1 week: Stephon Benjamin (Auburn), Jaray Rask (Iowa St., the reigning winner), Kevin Washington (Notre Dame), Thomas Teague (LSU), and Deshawn Bernard (Iowa St.). Molinich isn't among them at this earliest checkpoint — too soon to call it a snub with the season not yet played, but after two straight years of production without recognition, it's the first thing worth checking once Week 1 numbers are in.

The defense gets the bigger jump on paper — nine points, against six for the offense — which tracks with where the two 5-star transfers actually play. Whether that finally moves the run defense out of the national bottom 10 after three straight years stuck there is the single biggest question this preview can't answer yet.

SEGMENT 08

The Takeaway

This is, on paper, the most talented roster this program has ever put on the field — two 5-star transfers, a record signing class, and a returning core that includes the reigning national rushing leader and three All-Conference performers. The preseason rank hasn't caught up to any of that yet, still sitting at No. 89. If the ratings jump translates on the field, particularly on a defense that's needed it for three straight years, this could be the season this program finally breaks out of the range it's occupied since the very first tracked year.
