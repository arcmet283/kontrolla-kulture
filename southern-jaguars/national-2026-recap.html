SEGMENT 01

The Bracket That Ended In A Blowout

Voss
Play-by-Play
54-14, Renee. After a bracket full of tight finishes, the national championship game itself wasn't one of them. Walk me through how Texas and Georgia both got there.
Castellan
Analyst
Georgia entered unbeaten at 13-0 and the CFP's No. 1 overall seed, and their path there was a survival act — a 37-35 semifinal escape over Alabama in the Fiesta Bowl after Alabama had just knocked off Michigan 31-28 in the Orange Bowl. Texas's road ran through Clemson, 27-24 in the Peach Bowl semifinal, after Clemson had upset Texas Tech 48-34 in the Rose Bowl. Two semifinal classics setting up a title game that wasn't close at all.

And Georgia's only loss of the entire season came in that one game, at the worst possible time.

SEGMENT 05

The Coaching Carousel

Castellan
Analyst
Three jobs changed hands this cycle, all set up to debut in 2027: Oklahoma moves on from Brent Venables for Will Stein, promoted up from Oregon's offensive coordinator role. USC parts ways with Lincoln Riley for Jesse Minter, coming over from the Chargers' defensive staff. And Kentucky replaces Mark Stoops with Bob Surace out of Princeton.
Voss
Play-by-Play
Three job openings at three very different tiers of the sport — a blue blood in Oklahoma, a resurgent power in USC, and a rebuild in Kentucky. All three of those hires get their first test in 2027.
SEGMENT 06

What To Watch

Voss
Play-by-Play
Texas is the reigning champion heading into 2027, and Arch Manning's decorated season means he's exhausted a huge chunk of his eligibility clock already. What's the one thing carrying into next year for you?
Castellan
Analyst
Whether Georgia's response to a 40-point championship loss is a step forward or a step back — that's historically a real fork in the road for a program. And keep an eye on Southern's Molinich: a Doak Walker season as a true freshman on a team that didn't make a bowl is an unusual data point, and how the national conversation treats him going into year two is worth tracking.
