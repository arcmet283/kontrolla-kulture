A Loss At Texas, But Molinich Just Took Back Both Crowns

Record4–3

LastLost at No. 10 Texas, 27-45
Nextat Navy, Wk 8

The trip to Texas went about as expected on the scoreboard, but not on the stat sheet. Southern falls to 4–3 after a 27-45 loss to a top-10 team, yet Jordan Molinich turned in one of the best games of his career in a losing effort — and as of this week's national leaderboard, he's back on top of both major rushing categories at once. Southern stays 3–1 in the AAC since the loss was non-conference, still very much alive in the conference race heading into a road trip to Navy.

SEGMENT 01

The Week 7 Result: At No. 10 Texas

Week 7 · Away · Non-Conference
Texas 45, Southern 27 Lost

Foxworth struggled through the air, finishing 17-of-29 for 199 yards, no touchdowns, and an interception, and the defense gave up 348 passing yards to a ranked opponent. But Molinich was a one-man highlight reel: 223 rushing yards on just 21 carries and 3 touchdowns, plus 66 receiving yards. Jason Davis added 6 catches for 93 yards. It's the kind of game that reads as a moral split decision — a resounding loss on the scoreboard, but the best statistical outing of Southern's season from its best player, against by far its toughest opponent.

SEGMENT 02

Molinich Reclaims Both Crowns

National Rushing Leader · Yards AND Touchdowns
Jordan Molinich HB · JR
159 car, 1,173 yds, 13 TDs — 1st in the NCAA in both categories

Two weeks ago Molinich had fallen to 2nd nationally in rushing yards by just four yards. The Texas game alone was worth 223 more, and he's now the outright national leader in both rushing yards and rushing touchdowns — a clean sweep no other back in the country can claim right now. And yet: still not one of the sixteen names on the national Heisman Watchlist. A player leading the country in both major rushing categories getting zero recognition is a genuinely hard snub to explain away as a numbers problem at this point.

SEGMENT 04

What's Ahead

Next up is a road trip to Navy, currently 2–4 and 1–3 in the AAC. The only prior meeting between these two programs was a 24-31 home loss for Southern back in 2025, Year One of the rebuild — a completely different roster on both sides, and this year's game is on the road rather than at home. After Navy comes the season's final bye week, then a closing stretch of Temple, UAB, Memphis, and UTSA to finish the regular season.

SEGMENT 05

The Takeaway

4–3 with a signature statistical outing lost in a signature scoreboard loss, and a running back who just made a real case that he's the best player in the country regardless of what the Heisman list says. Navy on the road is a very winnable game against a team with a losing record — the kind of game that keeps this season's bowl math alive if Southern takes care of business.
