SEGMENT 05

CFP Implications: What Saturday Actually Decides

Zoom out, and the one-G5-bid rule is still the biggest structural fact in the room: whichever of Missouri State, Akron, Charlotte, or Arkansas State ends the weekend ranked highest among the group takes the only Group of 5 spot, locked to the No. 12 seed regardless of that ranking. Missouri State's the highest-ranked of that group entering the weekend, and a win over Kennesaw State keeps it that way.

The Georgia-Texas result reshuffles the top of the whole bracket no matter who wins. Whichever one loses drops behind UCLA, and possibly behind Missouri State's own 11-1 mark depending how the committee weighs a head-to-head loss against an unbeaten-outside-that-game résumé. That's the single most consequential result of the entire weekend for seeding purposes — more than any of the other nine games combined.

And if Southern wins its game, it's a first-ever conference championship — and probably not much more than that for the national picture. Southern's currently 27th, well outside the field regardless of the outcome. But for anyone following that broadcast directly, that's the whole season right there in one result.
