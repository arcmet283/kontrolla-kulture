"The Story So Far" — Title Card Set

Thirty-four cards, silent, styled for a 16:9 video frame — built with the real Southern Jaguars logos and brand colors, and to stand on their own with no pre-Army footage required. Each season gets a record card, a standout team-stat card, a full team-leaders board (passing, rushing, receiving, tackles, sacks, interceptions), a departed-players card, a signing-class card, and its own transfer-class card. The Stanford win gets its own beat before the four-game skid that followed it, the AAC Championship and the program's first-ever bowl game each get a dedicated matchup card with the actual Tulane, Gasparilla Bowl, and Tennessee logos. Every card has a slow built-in gold sheen and pulsing accent bar, so instead of a flat screenshot, screen-record each one for its listed hold time and drop the clips straight into your timeline in order. Cut on music; no highlight clips needed until Card 33 hard-cuts into your actual Army game footage.
