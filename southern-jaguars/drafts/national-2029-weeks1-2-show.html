The Defending Champion Is Already 0-For-1, And Texas Tech Is Already 0-2

Two weeks into the 2029 season and the preseason board already looks shaky. Eight ranked teams have lost already, seven of them to unranked opponents outright, the defending national champion is one of them, and a team that opened in the preseason polls is already 0-2 and out of the rankings entirely. This is the full national recap of Weeks 1 and 2, plus a first look at what Week 3 has waiting.

Eight ranked teams have lost through these two weeks, seven of them to unranked opponents outright. The single biggest of the bunch is Fresno State knocking off a preseason No. 10 Georgia team in Week 1 — and Georgia is no ordinary preseason No. 10, either: this program went 15-0 and won the SEC outright in 2026 before losing the national championship game itself to Texas, 54-14, the deepest and most recent CFP pedigree of any team in this upset list. Georgia has since fallen out of the rankings and had to rebuild against an unranked Georgia Tech in Week 2 (a 45-31 win) just to get back on track. LSU's loss to South Carolina carries its own real weight, too — LSU made the 2026 CFP as an 8-seed and beat UNLV in Round 1, 52-14, before its season ended; South Carolina's own case isn't nothing either, a 2026 Gator Bowl winner over Baylor, 31-13. Alabama has now been on both ends of the upset pattern within two weeks: upset by UAB in Week 1, then the team doing the upsetting themselves against a ranked Oklahoma team in Week 2, the only one of these eight results that was ranked-on-ranked.

SEGMENT 02

Florida State's Title Defense Is In Real Trouble

This chronicle flagged it all offseason: Florida State opened 2029 with the lowest team rating (80) of any program tracked on the national board, a full 17 points below preseason No. 1 Michigan, despite being the reigning national champion. Losing to Jacksonville State in Week 1 — a 37-31 defeat, with the Gamecocks' Jameson Scissum accounting for 248 total yards and 2 touchdowns — is exactly the kind of result that preseason number predicted. And this wasn't a nobody pulling the upset: Jacksonville State has won its bowl game in three straight tracked seasons (the 2025 Hawai'i Bowl, the 2026 Independence Bowl over UNC, and the 2027 Myrtle Beach Bowl), and its 2027 defensive end Michael Hallock swept the Lombardi Award as the national sack leader. No program in this chronicle has ever defended a national title. Two weeks in, Florida State looks like the least likely candidate yet to break that streak, against opponents with real credentials of their own.

SEGMENT 03

Texas Tech's Freefall

Preseason No. 22 with an 89 team rating, Texas Tech is already 0-2 and out of the polls entirely heading into Week 3 — a loss to No. 15 Texas A&M in Week 1 (42-36, Chris Sneed throwing for 283 yards and 5 touchdowns for the Aggies) started it, and a second loss has them staring at a road game against No. 7 UCLA in Week 3 with a real chance to open 0-3. This is a much steeper fall than "Big 12 contender" suggests: Joey McGuire's program was the 2025 national runner-up, losing the title game to Notre Dame 34-31 after beating Oklahoma and Miami to get there. From that peak it's been a slow slide — Big 12 champion outright in both 2026 and 2027, a CFP appearance in 2027, just a bowl trip in 2028, and now this. An 0-2 start is new territory for this program no matter how you measure it.

SEGMENT 04

The Statement Win: No. 1 Texas

Week 2
#1 Texas 45, #9 Tennessee 21

The preseason No. 1 team beating a ranked opponent by 24 is the single cleanest statement result of the young season — Brendon Haygood ran for 177 yards and 2 touchdowns on 29 carries. Texas is the rare preseason favorite that's actually played like one so far.

Elsewhere, No. 4 Notre Dame improved to 3-0 with a 38-28 win over then-No. 7 UNC, continuing an early bounce-back for a program that missed the CFP field entirely a year ago, and No. 8 UCLA needed overtime to get past No. 11 Wisconsin, 30-24.

SEGMENT 05

Heisman Watch, Early

Isaiah Mack (Penn St.) and Jordan Molinich (Southern) are tied for the most weeks logged on the board so far, four apiece. Notre Dame already has three separate names tracked (Weston Bryan, Tyrell Frye, Tay Brown) — a sign the Irish's turnaround is being noticed nationally beyond just the win column. It's an unusually receiver-heavy board early: six of the ten names tracked are wideouts, against just two quarterbacks and two running backs.

SEGMENT 06

What's Coming In Week 3

Nineteen games on the national board in Week 3, several of them ranked-on-ranked: No. 12 UNC hosts No. 21 Clemson, No. 16 Wisconsin visits Utah St., and No. 3 Penn State hosts No. 18 South Carolina. Texas Tech's 0-2 desperation and Florida State's already-shaky title defense both get real early tests too. A full separate preview covers the entire Week 3 slate, with real tracked history included for every team on it — not just the ranked ones.

SEGMENT 07

The Takeaway

Two weeks in, the preseason board already looks like it undersold the chaos coming. A defending champion that opened as the lowest-rated team tracked is already 0-for-1 on defending anything. A three-year CFP-or-title-game program is 0-2 and unranked. And the team that actually looks like its preseason billing, Texas, just posted the best win of the young season. If last year's pattern — two straight champions from outside the top four seeds — is going to make it three in a row, the early evidence says don't be surprised.
