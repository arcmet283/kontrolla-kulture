SEGMENT 01

Miami's Perfect Season Ends

Voss
Play-by-Play
Renee, Miami had been unbeaten and ranked No. 1 outright the last time we were on this desk. That's over now.
ON SCREEN: SYRACUSE DEF. #1 MIAMI, 28-20
Castellan
Analyst
An 8-point road upset by an unranked team is about as clean an upset as you'll see. Miami's now 6-1 and has dropped out of the top 10 in the media poll entirely, though the CFP committee is still showing them some respect at No. 10. However you slice it, the sport's last unbeaten blue blood just went down.
SEGMENT 02

Two Polls, Two Different No. 1 Teams

Voss
Play-by-Play
Here's the wrinkle with Miami now out of the picture: the CFP committee has Georgia at No. 1, but the media Top 25 has Penn State at No. 1. Both teams are 7-0.
Castellan
Analyst
Same record, different top team, depending on who's voting. Georgia sits 6th in the media poll despite being unbeaten and the committee's top team — that's a real gap in how these two bodies are weighing schedule strength or eye test right now. Worth watching whether that gap closes or widens as more results come in.

SEGMENT 05

Molinich Sweeps Both Rushing Crowns, Still No Heisman Buzz

Voss
Play-by-Play
We flagged Southern's Jordan Molinich a couple checkpoints back as the national rushing yards leader who'd just been passed by four yards. Update: he's taken it back, and then some.
Castellan
Analyst
1,173 rushing yards, 13 touchdowns, both of those numbers lead the entire country outright. That's a clean sweep of the sport's two marquee rushing categories, on a team that just lost to a ranked opponent on the road. And he is still not one of the sixteen names on the national Heisman Watchlist. At a certain point the absence itself is the headline — this isn't a compiling-numbers-on-a-bad-team case anymore, it's a back leading the country outright in both categories that matter most for his position.
