CFP #1 · Media #1UCLA9-0
Voss
Play-by-Play
The only unbeaten team left in either poll, unanimous No. 1 in both. Renee, what's actually behind that besides the record?
Castellan
Analyst
Less than you'd think, and that's what makes this team interesting to argue about. Zero presence in the Top 25 team recruiting boards across any of the three tracked cycles. They did land Sheriden Ducking, the No. 7 overall national high school recruit in the 2028 class, a safety out of Oregon — that's individual blue-chip talent that just doesn't show up when you only look at team-level rankings. And receiver Titus Mokiao-ati led the entire country in receiving touchdowns back in 2025, 15 of them. Beyond that, one bowl result on file: a 2026 Las Vegas Bowl win over Auburn, 21-17. No coach name on file for this program at all.

Play-by-Play
So the case against them isn't the record, it's the sample size of everything else.
Castellan
Analyst
Exactly. Nine wins is nine wins. But we have almost nothing else to compare it to.
CFP #2 · Media #6Georgia9-1
Voss
Play-by-Play
Georgia's the widest poll split among anybody in either top 10 — 2nd with the committee, 6th with the media, same 9-1 record. What's driving that?
Castellan
Analyst
History, probably. Georgia went 13-0 in the regular season in 2026, won the SEC, walked into the National Championship as a heavy favorite — and got run off the field by Texas, 54-14. The most lopsided title game this chronicle has tracked. That loss is still the single most useful data point for judging this year's team: proven they can get to the mountaintop, also proven they can get blown out once they arrive. Recruiting's been a fixture every cycle — 19th in 2026 behind Sam Williams, a 5-star linebacker; 5th in 2027 behind Braylon Staley, and that same class landed Connor Veach, the No. 5 overall national high school recruit that year; 17th in 2028 behind Donovan Murph. Kicker Peyton Woodring won the Lou Groza in 2026. No coach name on file.
Voss
Play-by-Play
Missouri State's gap is actually bigger, though — 18 spots to Georgia's 4.
Castellan
Analyst
Right, different category of disagreement — that one's top-10-versus-unranked. Georgia's is a fight within the top tier itself, which is arguably the more interesting one.
CFP #3 · Media #4Wisconsin8-1
Voss
Play-by-Play
8-1, no award winners, one recruiting board appearance — 18th in 2026 behind Rasheed Penniston. Thin file.
Castellan
Analyst
Thinner than it should look, actually, because there's real CFP pedigree buried in there. A No. 12 seed in 2025 that won its Round 1 game outright, 28-21 over Navy, before losing to Miami in Round 2, 35-28. So: a real scalp two years ago, trying to climb back to that level on an eight-game streak of its own right now. No coach name on file.
CFP #4 · Media #2Texas8-1
Voss
Play-by-Play
Texas is a past national champion, still rolling two years later. Walk me through the hardware.
Castellan
Analyst
2026 title, 54-14 over Georgia, capped a 15-1 season. Arch Manning swept the Heisman, Maxwell, and Davey O'Brien that year while breaking Texas's own single-season and career passing and touchdown records. Punter Dylan Lenners won the Ray Guy the same season, tight end Jack Endries won the Mackey — three major individual awards on one championship roster. And a year before that, defensive tackle Cole Brevard won the 2025 Lombardi. That's a national award winner in every tracked season leading into this one.
Voss
Play-by-Play
And the recruiting's kept pace?
Castellan
Analyst
16th in 2027, 4th in 2028 — sustained top-20 recruiting in the years right after a title, exactly what you'd expect from a staff cashing in. There's even a new name on this year's Heisman Watchlist, Ken Ward, a redshirt sophomore quarterback, 4 weeks logged. No coach name on file, but few programs in this field can point to a title this recent.

Recruiting's been a strength every cycle — 21st in 2026, 14th in 2027 behind Mike Mitchell, who was actually the No. 2 overall transfer in the country that cycle out of Notre Dame, and 14th again in 2028.
