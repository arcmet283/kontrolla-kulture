Southern Jaguars · AAC Championship Week · 2028 Season

8-4, A Doak-Walker-Caliber Season From Molinich, And A Trip To Chicago

Final Record8–4, 7–2 AAC
AAC Title Gameat Charlotte, Chicago, IL
Molinich2,203 Yds, 18 TD — Both #1 NCAA

The regular season is over, and this program is one win away from its first conference championship in the years this chronicle has tracked. Southern finishes 8-4, 7-2 in the AAC, and heads to a neutral site in Chicago to face Charlotte for the title. Jordan Molinich closed the year leading the entire country in both rushing yards and rushing touchdowns — a genuine Doak-Walker-caliber season, his second in three years — and is still not one of the eighteen names on the national Heisman Watchlist. This report covers the final stretch, the full-season numbers, and a real debate over Saturday's game.

SEGMENT 01

The Final Three Games

Week 11 · Home · AAC
UAB 17, Southern 13 Lost

The one true stumble of the closing stretch. Foxworth struggled badly, 9-of-27 for 93 yards and an interception, and Molinich's 245 yards on 28 carries wasn't enough to overcome it. UAB entered the day well under .500 — the kind of loss that stings more in hindsight than it looked like it should going in.

Week 12 · Home · AAC
Southern 42, Memphis 35 Won

Foxworth was benched again after an 0-for-6 start, and true freshman James Moody stepped in and delivered: 14-of-22 for 188 yards and 4 touchdowns. Molinich added 134 yards and 2 scores on 23 carries, and Gavin Hoffman caught 2 touchdowns of his own. The quarterback competition that looked settled in October is clearly not settled at all.

Week 13 · Home · AAC
Southern 10, UTSA 2 Won

A defensive slugfest to close the regular season. Moody left in the second quarter with an injury; Foxworth relieved him and went 9-of-14 for 145 yards and a touchdown. Molinich ground out 107 yards on 25 carries in a game with almost no points to go around. Christian Thatcher had his best game of the season on defense: 10 tackles, a tackle for loss, and 2 forced fumbles.

SEGMENT 02

Molinich's Finished Product

National Rushing Leader · Yards AND Touchdowns, Full Season
Jordan Molinich HB · JR
285 car, 2,203 yds, 18 TDs — 1st in the NCAA in both categories, final regular-season totals

2,203 rushing yards falls short of his own freshman-year program record (2,349 in 2026), but it's the more impressive number in context — a full season carrying an offense that changed starting quarterbacks four separate times. This is his third consecutive season leading the nation in rushing yards outright — 2,349 in 2026, 1,669 in 2027, and now 2,203 in 2028, a streak this chronicle hasn't seen from any other player. He swept both rushing categories in 2026 and again this year; 2027 was the one exception, when Fluff Bothwell of Mississippi State led the nation in rushing touchdowns instead. And once again: not one of the eighteen names on the Heisman Watchlist. Three straight years leading the country in rushing yards, zero Heisman recognition in any of them.

SEGMENT 06

The Takeaway

8-4 doesn't look like much on its own, but this is a program that just played its way into a conference championship game behind the best individual season in its own history and a quarterback room that kept finding a way through injuries and benchings all year long. Win or lose Saturday, this is the best season this chronicle has tracked for Southern Jaguars football. A win makes it the program's first-ever conference title.
