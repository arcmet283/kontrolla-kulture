Notre Dame won the national title and turned around to sign the No. 1 recruiting class in the country that same cycle — the cleanest possible confirmation that winning it all is worth exactly what recruiting departments always claim it's worth. Texas Tech, the runner-up, landed at No. 3. But look at the four Round 1 upsets more closely: in two of them, the team that actually lost the football game (Clemson, No. 10; Alabama, No. 6) carried a better recruiting class into the next cycle than the team that beat them (Texas, unranked; Indiana, No. 24). Winning the game and winning the recruiting battle that follows it are not the same contest, and for half of this bracket's first round, they went to different teams entirely.

SEGMENT 07

Southern's First Bowl Trip

2026 Gasparilla Bowl
Tennessee 44, Southern 31 Lost

The first bowl appearance in the tracked history of this program, one year after USC won this exact same bowl over Colorado 44–41. Starting QB Joshua Clark was injured in the first quarter, missed the next two quarters, then returned in the fourth; backup Ty Wilson filled in and threw for 188 yards and a touchdown. The recruiting class already on Southern's roster that season (signed for 2026, ranked No. 59 nationally) sat well outside the Top 25, and the class signed immediately after this bowl trip (for 2027) landed at No. 63 — consistent with the wider pattern in this ledger that a first bowl trip rarely coincides with a Top 25 class, either the one already on the roster or the one that follows.

SEGMENT 09

Does Recruiting Rank Predict Bowl Season?

Pulling every data point from Segments 2 and 4 together, more strongly than expected: of the eight teams that won at least one 2025 CFP game (Oklahoma, Texas, Indiana, Wisconsin, Miami, Penn St., Notre Dame, Texas Tech), seven carried a Top 25 class into the very next cycle — Texas, an actual Round 1 winner over Clemson, is the lone team that didn't. Of the six teams that won at least one 2026 CFP game (LSU, Notre Dame, Clemson, Alabama, Texas, Georgia), all six did. Across two tracked seasons, that's 13 of 14 CFP-game-winning programs landing a Top 25 class the following cycle. Recruiting rank doesn't just correlate with CFP success in this data — on this evidence, it's close to a precondition for it. The real surprise isn't which teams recruit well and win; it's that Texas managed to win playoff games in 2025 without one.

SEGMENT 10

What's Left: 2027

Every bowl slot for 2027 is still blank, waiting on conference championship weekend to finish before selection can even begin. Southern is bowl-eligible for the second straight year at 6–6 and waiting on its bid. Texas Tech and Iowa State play for a Big 12 title and a near-certain top playoff seed. And Mike Uremovich's Ball State, three years after this chronicle's very first individual coaching award, is one win away from a title-game win that's eluded him ever since — and still chasing his first bowl win to go with it.
