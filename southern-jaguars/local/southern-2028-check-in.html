The first three games were a combined 123–36. USF was a different story from the opening snap — a reigning AAC champion that made Southern look like a team playing its first real road test of the year for the second week in a row, and this time the result matched the difficulty.

SEGMENT 02

Foxworth Hits A Wall

Starting Quarterback
Joseph Foxworth QB · SO
49-of-97, 716 yds, 8 TD, 2 Int

Three weeks of a clean sophomore leap — zero interceptions, eight touchdowns — ran into USF's defense and didn't survive it. Foxworth went 5-of-19 for 107 yards with 2 interceptions before getting pulled in the third quarter, the first real stumble of his season. James Moody came off the bench and was sharper in relief, going 7-of-18 for 165 yards and 2 touchdowns. Nothing's been announced about who starts at ECU, but this is the first time all season that question is actually open.

There's a strange symmetry to it. The last time these two teams played, in 2027, it was Moody getting benched against ECU and a true freshman named Joseph Foxworth coming off the bench to run the offense for the first time in his career. A season later, against that same opponent, Foxworth is the one walking back to the sideline and Moody's the one finishing the game — and whether this rematch settles anything the way the last one did is the whole subplot of the week.

SEGMENT 03

Molinich's Workload

Through 4 Games
Jordan Molinich HB · JR
685 rush yds, 7 TD · 72 rec yds, 3 TD

112 rushing yards and 2 touchdowns against Kentucky, 226 and 2 more against North Texas, 222 rushing yards and 3 touchdowns at Army, then 125 more at USF — 685 yards on the ground through four games, plus 72 receiving yards and 3 receiving touchdowns on top of it. The preview's prediction was a decline toward 1,300–1,500 yards on the season as the offense balances out; this pace, if it held, would blow well past that. The one thing USF's defense couldn't take away was the run game — Molinich still got his, even in a loss.

SEGMENT 04

The Defense: The Question Got Answered

Three weeks of a defense that hadn't been tested against the run ended with an answer nobody wanted: USF gave up 276 rushing yards against it. The preview's biggest question — whether the nine-point ratings jump actually moved this unit out of the national bottom 10 — looks like it didn't, at least not against a real AAC run game. Through three weeks the box scores simply hadn't forced the issue; USF forced it immediately, and the defense didn't hold.

It wasn't a total collapse. Christian Thatcher's 17 tackles and 4 sacks through three games and Ricardo Jones' two interceptions were real positives earlier in the season, and neither got worse at USF — the run defense problem specifically is what showed up. ECU finished 2027 at 5–7, 3–6 in the AAC, a step down from USF's level. Whether that's a chance to patch the problem against lesser competition, or whether it shows up again regardless of opponent, is the question this week actually answers.

SEGMENT 05

What's Ahead

Week 5 · Road · AAC Play · Southern Leads, 2–1
at ECU

Southern's won two of three in this series, but the most recent meeting is the one that matters this week: 2027, a 52–14 loss at home that's remembered less for the score and more for what it kicked off — Moody getting pulled and a true freshman named Joseph Foxworth taking the offense for the first time in his career. A season later the roles are reversed, Foxworth's the one who got pulled, and it's happening against the same team. ECU finished 2027 at 5–7, 3–6 in the AAC — a step down from USF, and exactly the kind of game a shaken quarterback room needs to find its footing again. The pick here: Southern, and probably the start of an answer at quarterback either way.

SEGMENT 06

The Takeaway

3–1, 2–1 in AAC play, a quarterback who just had his first bad week, and a run defense that finally got tested and lost that test. USF was the toughest opponent on the schedule so far, and it showed everywhere at once — the passing game, the run defense, even who's starting at quarterback. ECU is next, a lesser team on paper but a loaded matchup anyway, given what happened the last time these two played. This is where the season either steadies back out or starts to unravel.
