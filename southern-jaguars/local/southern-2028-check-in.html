The first two wins came at home; Army was the first real road test, and the first opponent that's actually given this program trouble before — a 59–6 blowout loss at home in 2025, even after Southern won both meetings since. This time it wasn't close: 44–12, a combined 123–36 across three games now. USF, the reigning AAC champion, is next, and it's a different kind of test entirely.

SEGMENT 02

Foxworth's Clean Start

Starting Quarterback
Joseph Foxworth QB · SO
44-of-78, 609 yds, 8 TD, 0 Int

Foxworth opened the year 21-of-33 for 260 yards and 4 touchdowns against Kentucky, followed it with a more efficient 13-of-24 for 148 yards and 2 more scores against North Texas, then went 10-of-21 for 201 yards and 2 more touchdowns at Army. Three games, eight total touchdowns, zero interceptions — exactly the sophomore leap the preview flagged as the most likely development-curve pattern in this program's own data, and it's held up through the first true road test.

SEGMENT 03

Molinich's Workload

Through 3 Games
Jordan Molinich HB · JR
560 rush yds, 7 TD · 72 rec yds, 3 TD

112 rushing yards and 2 touchdowns against Kentucky, 226 rushing yards and 2 more against North Texas, then 222 rushing yards and 3 touchdowns at Army — no receiving stats this time, but 560 yards on the ground through three games, plus 72 receiving yards and 3 receiving touchdowns banked from the first two weeks. The preview's prediction was a decline toward 1,300–1,500 yards on the season as the offense balances out; this pace, if it held, would blow well past that. Three games is still early, but it's the opposite direction the preview expected.

SEGMENT 04

The Defense: Still Unproven Against The Run

The biggest question from the preview — whether the defense's nine-point ratings jump finally moves the run defense out of the national bottom 10 — still doesn't have a hard-numbers answer. Southern held Army to just 12 points on the road, which is an encouraging scoreboard result, but a specific rushing-yards-allowed figure isn't tracked for this one the way it is for some other games this season, so it's not being called a confirmed turnaround off one result. What is confirmed: Christian Thatcher added 7 tackles and 2 more sacks at Army, giving him multi-sack games in two of the first three weeks, and Ricardo Jones picked off a pass for the third straight game — the clearest sign yet that the secondary addition is exactly what it was billed as. The run defense's real test is still ahead.

SEGMENT 05

What's Ahead

Week 4 · Road · USF Leads, 1–0
at USF

USF is the reigning AAC champion, a 2027 CFP participant, and the only team to beat Southern the last time these two met (14–21 back in 2025). Second straight road trip, and by far the toughest test of the season so far.

SEGMENT 06

The Takeaway

3–0, a quarterback who still hasn't turned it over, and a running back pacing well ahead of his own preseason projection. Army was supposed to be the first real road test, and Southern passed it comfortably. USF is a different question entirely — the reigning AAC champion, and the toughest game this program has faced since the season opened.
