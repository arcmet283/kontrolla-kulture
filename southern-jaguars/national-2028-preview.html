Voss
And it's not just Texas. UCLA carries an 89 overall rating — same number as Notre Dame at No. 1 and Michigan at No. 5 — and they're sitting at No. 25.
Castellan
Right, so this poll clearly isn't a straight sort of that rating column. There's returning production, schedule strength, program pedigree, probably last year's finish, all baked in on top of it. Which is a good reminder for anybody using these ratings to make their own picks — the number alone doesn't tell the whole story the committee's actually using.
Voss
Notre Dame's dead-even across the board — 89 overall, 89 offense, 89 defense. Perfectly balanced, no obvious weakness.
Castellan
No real weakness to attack on paper. Compare that to Nebraska at No. 12 — a 95 offense paired with an 82 defense. That's a team that's going to win a lot of shootouts and probably lose at least one game it shouldn't.
SEGMENT 02

Ole Miss: The Champion's Encore

Voss
Ole Miss opens at No. 2 after that stunning 7-seed title run. Not No. 1, though — that's interesting on its own.
Castellan
We've actually seen this pattern before on this desk. Notre Dame won it all in 2025 and took a real step back the very next season. Texas won it in 2026 and their recruiting class the following cycle was a genuine step down too. Defending a title in this sport, in this data, has never once meant returning as the unquestioned favorite. Ole Miss at No. 2 fits right into that pattern.

What To Watch

Voss
Renee, close us out. What's the one thing you want eyes on as this season kicks off?
Castellan
Whether that ratings-versus-ranking gap we opened with actually means anything once games are played. If Texas at No. 16 starts blowing out teams the way a 91-rated roster should, that's a real story about this preseason poll undervaluing somebody. And keep half an eye on Iowa — a coach with that much history landing on a hot-seat board for the first time is the kind of storyline that either fizzles by October or completely dominates a season.
