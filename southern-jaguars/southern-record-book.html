The Trophy Case

2025
Mark Fiaseu 2nd Team All-American

1st Team All-Conference; also 3 National Offensive Player of the Week awards (Weeks 5, 12, 13) and 1 Conference POTW — the program's first All-American recognition in this chronicle's record.

2026 · The Breakout
Jordan Molinich Doak Walker Award

The nation's best running back, as a true freshman — the program's first and only national award to date. Also 1st Team All-American, Freshman All-American, and 1st Team All-Conference the same season.

2027 Preseason & Season
Jordan Molinich Preseason All-American

1st Team All-Conference for the season — his third straight season carrying some form of All-American recognition.

2028 Preseason
Jordan Molinich 2nd Team All-American (Preseason)

Molinich has now been tabbed an All-American honoree in some form in every season he's played for this program — 2026, 2027, and entering 2028.

The program has produced exactly one national award winner in this chronicle's record: Molinich's 2026 Doak Walker Award. No Southern player has been drafted after an All-American season yet — Brennon Fonua and Mark Fiaseu both went to the NFL Draft out of the 2025 class, before the program's rating ever cracked 65, and Molinich himself hasn't left college yet.

SECTION 12

Still Waiting On

Four seasons in, here's the list of program firsts still unclaimed: a winning conference-title-game or bowl-game result (0-2 in both so far), a win over a ranked opponent (0-2), a Heisman Trophy or Heisman finalist appearance, back-to-back winning seasons, and a repeat leader at any offensive skill position. Molinich's Doak Walker Award, his All-American recognition streak, and the 2027 recruiting jump are the clearest signs of a program on the rise — the win-loss record just hasn't caught all the way up yet.
