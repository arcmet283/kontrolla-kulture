Cold Open: The Collapse

Voss
Play-by-Play
Renee, I've done a lot of these openings. I don't think I've ever opened one with a team that started the year ranked No. 1 in the country and is now sitting outside the Top 25 entirely, two weeks later.
Castellan
Analyst
Notre Dame lost to Stanford in Week 0, then lost to Ohio in Week 1. Two losses, zero wins, and a preseason team rating that had them perfectly balanced — 89 offense, 89 defense, 89 overall. On paper this was the most complete team in the country. On the field, it's 0 and 2.
ON SCREEN: WEEK 0 — STANFORD DEF. #1 NOTRE DAME, 45-35 · WEEK 1 — OHIO DEF. #11 NOTRE DAME, 31-21
Voss
Role
And it's worth noting, they were still ranked 11th heading into that Ohio loss — the committee gave them the benefit of the doubt after one bad week. Didn't survive a second.
SEGMENT 02

Notre Dame, Three Years In Context

Castellan
Analyst
This is what makes it stunning instead of just bad, Derek. We've tracked this program for three full seasons. 2025, national champion. 2026, CFP Round 2 exit — a step back, but still a playoff team. 2027, they made the semifinal round before losing. Three straight years of being in the championship conversation. This is the first time in that span they've missed the field before October even arrives.
Voss
Play-by-Play
So what actually broke? Was this predictable?
Castellan
Analyst
Not from the ratings — a team doesn't post an 89/89/89 profile by accident, that's real roster strength. This looks like it's about the schedule they walked into, not the roster itself. Losing to Stanford and Ohio isn't a talent gap story, not with ratings that high. It's two bad weeks from a team that had zero margin for error at the top of the poll. We flagged in the preseason show that Notre Dame's balanced profile made them dangerous but not obviously the best team in the country — Arizona State had the identical 80/80/80 balance a tier down. This is exactly the kind of poll-position fragility that comment was getting at.

SEGMENT 05

Florida State's Quiet 2-0

Voss
Play-by-Play
Florida State is the only 2-0 team we've got, and they're ranked 12th — lower than three separate 1-0 teams. Talk me through that.
Castellan
Analyst
It just means the committee is weighing quality of win over quantity of wins this early, which is normal in a two-game sample. Worth watching whether they climb if that record holds — a perfect record with no signature win yet is a different profile than what Ole Miss or Alabama are building at 1-0.
SEGMENT 06

Conference Streak Watch: Miami & Texas Tech

Castellan
Analyst
This is the one I'm watching most closely all year. Miami and Texas Tech have each won their conference championship three straight years running, 2025 through 2027 — nobody else in the sport can say that. Both are 1-0 to open this season, both sitting comfortably inside the current Top 10. If either one wins their conference again, that's four straight, in a sport where we've watched multiple coaching staffs turn over entirely in that same window.
Voss
Play-by-Play
And Texas Tech's Joey McGuire has been building to this the whole time — title game runner-up in 2025, then the Big 12 championship and a Best Head Coach Award in 2026, then back to the conference final again in 2027. If he gets Texas Tech there a fourth time, that's as complete a résumé as anyone in the country is building right now.
