Castellan
A win's a win, but a three-point margin over an in-state rival isn't the kind of statement that quiets questions about a roster that's been bleeding transfers for three straight years. Two weeks in, that story's not settled either way.

Notre Dame's 0-3 now, Renee. SMU got them in Week 2, and they've fallen all the way out of the rankings. The preseason No. 1 is unranked three weeks into the season.
Castellan
That's not a slow start anymore, that's a full-blown crisis. Three losses to open the year for a team we had dead-even with no weaknesses. Whatever we missed in the preview, we missed it everywhere.

And it's not just Notre Dame. Preseason No. 2 Alabama lost to No. 13 Texas in Week 2, then lost again to Boise State in Week 3.
Castellan
Two straight losses for a team we had picked near the very top of the country. The first one's at least explainable, a ranked loss to a quality Texas team. Losing to Boise State the very next week is a different conversation entirely.

Oregon's second loss looked nothing like the first. Week 0 was a 9-14 defensive slugfest against Michigan State. Week 2 was a 38-31 shootout loss to No. 1 Ole Miss.

Two completely different ways to lose a football game, same result both times. That's not one bad matchup, that's a team that hasn't found an identity that travels.

And Week 3 had three more ranked teams go down outright: No. 3 Michigan to No. 17 Wisconsin, No. 9 Ohio State to No. 18 UCLA, and No. 8 Texas Tech to No. 14 Texas A&M.
Castellan
Three weeks into this season, and the only safe prediction left is that nobody in the preseason Top 10 should feel safe. We'll keep tracking it as it comes.
