The Bracket That Crowned Notre Dame

Voss
Play-by-Play
Welcome to the very first National Desk recap on record, Renee. Notre Dame closes it out 34-31 over Texas Tech for the national championship. Walk me through how they got there.
Castellan
Analyst
A four-team bracket that got there the hard way. Indiana knocked out Alabama 49-38 in the first round, Miami handled Wisconsin in the Orange Bowl, and Notre Dame needed a semifinal win over Penn State, 52-28, before finishing off Texas Tech in the title game. Texas Tech, for what it's worth, beat Oklahoma 39-33 in the Sugar Bowl and then Miami 35-23 in the Fiesta Bowl semifinal just to get to that final.

SEGMENT 05

The Coaching Carousel Starts Spinning

Castellan
Analyst
Six programs made coaching changes this offseason, and this is where the carousel we've been tracking ever since actually starts: Arkansas moves on from Sam Pittman for Jake Spavital, Florida swaps Billy Napier for Dana Dimel, Maryland brings in Andy Kotelnicki, Oklahoma State hires Ryan Walters, UAB goes with Tom Stepsis, and Virginia turns to Adam Fuller. All six take over for the 2026 season.
Voss
Play-by-Play
Worth flagging early: Maryland's Kotelnicki beats No. 2 Michigan 30-27 in his Season 2, and Florida's Dimel knocks off No. 5 Texas 45-20 in his debut year. Two hires that look sharp out of the gate.
