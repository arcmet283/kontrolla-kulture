Through six games, Southern was 1–4 and searching for a quarterback. Five games later, they're 5–5, winners of three straight, and one win away from a second consecutive bowl trip. This is the check-in: how the turnaround happened, where Southern sits heading into the two games left on the schedule, and what's still exactly the same as it's always been.

SEGMENT 01

The Turnaround

Southern opened 1–4 with three different quarterbacks cycling through the lineup. Since true freshman Joseph Foxworth took over full-time in Week 7, the record is 4–1 — the only blemish a Week 8 loss to Tulsa. The last three games have all been wins.

SEGMENT 03

Foxworth's Emergence

A 2-star true freshman who signed outside the position's top 200 nationally is now Southern's starting quarterback and its Team Passing Leader: 102-of-185, 1,627 yards, 16 touchdowns, 6 interceptions. Since claiming the job in Week 6, he's thrown for at least 3 touchdowns in three of his five starts and hasn't taken the offense out of the top half of the conference in scoring since.

SEGMENT 04

Molinich, Still The Nation's Best

Jordan Molinich leads the country in rushing yards for the second straight season — his entire college career, both years: 190 carries, 1,416 yards, 10 touchdowns — 1st in the NCAA for rushing yards, 3rd in the AAC for rushing touchdowns. He hasn't missed a beat since last year's Doak Walker season.

SEGMENT 07

Heisman Watch: A Stall, Not A Fade

Molinich remains on the Heisman Watchlist at 4 weeks — the same total he carried into the Week 10 check-in. That means he hasn't been re-added to a single poll in the two weeks since, even during a stretch where he's kept leading the country in rushing. The players around him haven't stalled: Bryce Underwood is up to 7 weeks, Richard Young and Fluff Bothwell are both at 8, and Iowa State's Jaray Rask has climbed to 6. Production alone isn't moving Molinich's national profile right now.

SEGMENT 08

What's Left

Two games remain: at Memphis this week, then at UTSA to close the regular season. Southern needs one more win to reach six and qualify for a bowl game for the second year in a row — and Memphis, sitting at the exact same 5–5 record, is as even a swing game as the schedule could have handed them. A win Saturday clinches bowl eligibility with a game to spare.
