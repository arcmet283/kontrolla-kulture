SEGMENT 01

Notre Dame: 0-3, And It's Getting Worse

Voss
Play-by-Play
Renee, when we opened the season Notre Dame was the preseason No. 1, a perfectly balanced 89 on offense, 89 on defense. Three games later, they're 0-3. This week it was SMU, 38-31.
Castellan
Analyst
Stanford in Week 0, Ohio in Week 1, now SMU in Week 2. Three losses to three very different kinds of opponents — that rules out a single bad matchup as the explanation. This is a program-wide problem, not a scheduling quirk.

Play-by-Play
And here's the part that should really worry that fan base: Kevin Washington is still sitting on their Heisman Watchlist, three weeks running, on an 0-3 team. That tells you the roster still has individual talent. The results just aren't showing it.
SEGMENT 02

The Blue-Blood Bloodbath

Notre Dame isn't the only traditional power in freefall. Alabama and Ohio State have both lost twice in the last two weeks alone.

Week 2-3
Alabama: Back-To-Back Losses

Lost to No. 13 Texas 31-28 in Week 2, then to unranked Boise State 38-31 in Week 3. From a preseason contender to 2-2 and 17th in the country in the span of two weeks.

Week 3-4
Ohio State: Back-To-Back Losses

Fell to No. 18 UCLA 35-21 in Week 3, then to unranked Washington 32-17 in Week 4. Now 1-2 and clinging to the back of the rankings at No. 23.

Castellan
Analyst
Notre Dame, Alabama, Ohio State — three of the sport's most decorated programs are a combined 4-8 right now. Whatever's driving the chaos this season, it isn't sparing anybody at the top.
SEGMENT 03

SMU's Surprise Unbeaten Run

Voss
Play-by-Play
While all that's happening at the top, SMU has quietly gone 5-0 — including that win over Notre Dame — and they're sitting at just No. 19.
Castellan
Analyst
A 5-0 team ranked outside the top 15 is unusual, and it says the committee wants to see more before buying all the way in — fair, given the résumé is built on beating a struggling Notre Dame team rather than a murderer's row. But if they keep winning, that ranking has to catch up eventually.
SEGMENT 04

Texas Tech's Streak, Broken

Voss
Play-by-Play
We flagged Texas Tech a few weeks back as one of two programs chasing a fourth straight conference title, Miami being the other. That got a lot more complicated this week.
Castellan
Analyst
No. 14 Texas A&M beat No. 8 Texas Tech 49-35 — Tech's first loss of the season, dropping them to 2-1 and down to 12th in the country. The four-peat bid isn't dead, a Week 3 non-conference-or-early loss doesn't eliminate a Big 12 run, but it's the first real crack in that streak we've tracked. Miami, meanwhile, is still unbeaten at 4-0 and holding at No. 3 — their path looks a lot cleaner right now.

Eleven unbeaten teams remain in the Top 25 — Ole Miss, UNC, Miami, Iowa State, LSU, Penn State, Florida State, Wisconsin, UCLA, Georgia, and SMU. That's an unusually large unbeaten group this deep into the season, and it's a big part of why this year has felt so chaotic: normally by Week 5 the field has already started separating itself. Ole Miss, the reigning national champion, has held the No. 1 spot all season so far.

SEGMENT 06

Heisman Watch: A Crowded, Wide-Open Field

Voss
Play-by-Play
Twelve names deep on the national Heisman Watchlist now, and it's almost entirely quarterbacks and running backs from the unbeaten or one-loss crowd — Stephon Benjamin at Auburn, Jaray Rask at Iowa State, AK Dear at Ole Miss, a pair from surprise-unbeaten SMU in Andre Taukolo and Derrick McFall.
Castellan
Analyst
And worth flagging for anyone following along with the Southern Jaguars broadcast: their running back Jordan Molinich leads the entire NCAA in rushing yards right now, 685 through four games, and he's still not on this list. That's not a knock on this watchlist specifically — production from a rebuilding program outside the rankings just doesn't get the same early attention nationally. But if he keeps this pace, that's going to get harder to ignore.

Five weeks in, and we've already had more blue-blood chaos than most full seasons produce. Buckle up.
