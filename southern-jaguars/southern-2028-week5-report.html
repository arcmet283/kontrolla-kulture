SEGMENT 02

The Statement: 44-12 At Army

Week 3 · Away
Southern 44, Army 12 Won

Foxworth went 10-of-21 for 201 yards and 2 touchdowns without an interception. Molinich exploded for 222 rushing yards and 3 touchdowns. Jonathan Truss added 92 yards and a score, Hoffman chipped in a touchdown of his own, and Christian Thatcher and Ricardo Jones each made a defensive play (7 tackles/2 sacks and an interception, respectively). A 32-point road win over a program Southern has had a rocky history with.

SEGMENT 03

The Gut-Check: 23-45 At USF

Week 4 · Away · AAC
USF 45, Southern 23 Lost

The first real bad week of the season. Foxworth struggled and was pulled in the 3rd quarter, finishing 5-of-19 for 107 yards with 2 interceptions. Moody came in and actually outplayed him statistically (7-of-18, 165 yards, 2 TDs). Molinich still ran for 125 yards but didn't find the end zone. Hoffman had a strong day (109 receiving yards, 1 TD) that got buried by the final score. The bigger issue was the defense, which surrendered 276 rushing yards.

SEGMENT 04

Foxworth's First Real Bench

This is worth sitting with. Foxworth has been the season's headline story — the 2-star, rank-215 afterthought who beat out two other quarterbacks last year and opened 2028 completing 63.6% of his passes. The USF benching is his first real red flag as a starter, and notably, Moody — the same quarterback he originally leapfrogged in 2027 — is the one who relieved him and played better in the moment. Whether this becomes a real competition again or was a one-game hiccup is the biggest storyline to watch over the next few weeks.

SEGMENT 05

Molinich: Still Leading The Country, Still Off The Watchlist

Molinich is the NCAA's leading rusher through four weeks — 685 yards on just 96 carries, 7 touchdowns, an offensive Player of the Week nod for his 222-yard, 3-touchdown day at Army. He is still not on the Heisman Watchlist, now 12 names deep. This is the fourth straight checkpoint across three seasons where this exact pattern has repeated. At some point the absence itself becomes the story.

SEGMENT 06

The Pritchett Injury

The one real bad-news item off the field: Todd Pritchett, the 4-star linebacker from the 2027 signing class, tore a shoulder muscle in the USF game and is out for the season. He was a legitimate developmental piece (75 to 77 overall through his first two tracked seasons) and this is the first significant season-ending injury this program has dealt with since this chronicle began tracking.

The Takeaway

3-1 with a signature road win, a signature bad week, and now a real injury to account for is about as normal a start as a rising program can have — the difference from prior seasons is that this team has the ceiling (Molinich's national-best rushing, Foxworth's efficiency, a defense that's shown flashes) to make 3-1 feel like a floor instead of a peak. ECU on the road is the next real test of whether that's true.
